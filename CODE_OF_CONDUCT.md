# Code of conduct

This project wants to be a friendly place to learn, ask questions and
contribute, whatever your experience, background, language or the size of
your contribution. A first pull request that fixes a typo is as welcome as a
new feature.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and remember that many people
  here write in their second or third language.
- Give feedback on the work, not on the person. Explain the why, suggest
  an alternative, and say what is good as well.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change. Ask for the reason if it is
  not clear; it is usually about scope or maintenance cost.
- Keep discussions on topic and in public threads where possible, so others
  can learn from them.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks, in any channel.
- Discriminatory jokes or language about anyone's identity, body, ability,
  age, religion, nationality or background.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after a decision, or pinging people to get
  attention.

## Scope

This applies to the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and any other
place where you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer,
Saizul Amin, through the email address on the
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/saizul), or use GitHub's
"Report content" option. Reports are handled confidentially. You will get a
reply within a week.

## Consequences

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, and temporarily or
permanently block people who do not follow this code. In most cases the
first step is a private, friendly reminder.

These guidelines are inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
